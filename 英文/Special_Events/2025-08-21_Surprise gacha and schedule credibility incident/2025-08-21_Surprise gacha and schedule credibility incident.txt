【2025-08-21 Surprise gacha and schedule credibility incident】

Date: 2025-08-21
Event: Surprise gacha and schedule credibility incident


The official August schedule listed only a Miku event and a Qixi live on the 23rd, but on August 21 a gacha was suddenly added. Unlike the same period on JP, this gacha removed normal limited cards (only permanent and fes-limited cards), narrowing the selection range. After the event ended on August 25, top-ranked players renamed themselves in protest. The August 31 Miku birthday event announcement included a 'subject to change without notice' clause. (Source: NGA community public threads)

## 数据来源

Crypton Future Media, Inc. is a sound-software company founded by Hiroyuki Itoh in July 1995 in Chuo-ku, Sapporo, Hokkaido, starting as an importer of audio materials. It released MEIKO in 2004 and KAITO in 2006, then launched Hatsune Miku on August 31, 2007, the first of its Character Vocal Series, based on the VOCALOID2 engine. Crypton operates the PIAPRO creation platform and the KARENT music label, holds the annual MAGICAL MIRAI live event and SNOW MIKU festival, and has run the MIKU EXPO world tour since 2014. In this title, Crypton participates from the planning stage in a role close to a producer, as the character rights holder and voicebank developer of the Piapro Characters. (Sources: Crypton official site, SEGA press releases, public encyclopedia references)

# 2022-11-01 Connect Live guideline change and repost ban discussion

- **Date**：2022-11-01
- **Event**：Connect Live guideline change and repost ban discussion
- **Subject**：Global

The JP server revised the Connect Live (virtual live) usage guidelines to explicitly prohibit reposting and redistribution on public platforms; the Global account issued the same warning in four languages, including Japanese. As the Global server had never officially launched Connect Live, the notice drew widespread mockery and discussion.

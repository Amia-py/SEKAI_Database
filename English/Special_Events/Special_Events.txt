【Special Events Record】


---- Date | Event | Details ----
2025-03-27 | Mainland China launch | The Mainland China edition launched. As it lagged roughly four years behind the JP server, it started directly from the JP 3rd-anniversary (3.5-year) version, skipping the first three years of events and gachas; event stories remained viewable at any time, with gachas re-run per the operator's schedule. (Source: NGA community public threads)
2025-04 | Catch-up pace and event cadance complaints | After launch, the CN server rapidly caught up with other servers by running events back-to-back at half the JP duration, while event shop costs remained unchanged and no stamina was granted, sharply reducing effective rewards; the official Bilibili comment sections saw large-scale player complaints. (Source: NGA community public threads)
2025-04 | Pass differential treatment controversy | Players noted that although the CN and TW servers are both operated by Nuverse, the TW server's pass could be exchanged with free crystals while the CN pass required payment, citing differential treatment under the same publisher. (Source: NGA community public threads)
2025-08-02 | Recruit sticker auto-conversion bug | A severe bug caused gacha recruit stickers to be automatically converted into wish fragments. On August 2 the operator issued a final announcement stating it would re-issue consumed stickers to affected players and would not recall the converted fragments, but no additional compensation was mentioned, drawing player discontent. (Source: TapTap community reports)
2025-08-21 | Surprise gacha and schedule credibility incident | The official August schedule listed only a Miku event and a Qixi live on the 23rd, but on August 21 a gacha was suddenly added. Unlike the same period on JP, this gacha removed normal limited cards (only permanent and fes-limited cards), narrowing the selection range. After the event ended on August 25, top-ranked players renamed themselves in protest. The August 31 Miku birthday event announcement included a 'subject to change without notice' clause. (Source: NGA community public threads)
2025-08-26 | Rhythm-game notes disappearing bug | Around 21:00-22:00, some players found the falling notes in the rhythm game had disappeared (only part of the judgment bar visible), severely impacting play. Major bugs had occurred for three consecutive days around this time. (Source: NGA community public threads)
2025-09 | Minor spending incident | A 12-year-old child made 15 in-app purchases totaling 1,086 CNY in a single day on the CN server, drawing public attention to the handling of minors' spending. (Source: SMZDM community reports)
2026-04-09 | 'Turning Pain into Drive' event data anomaly and compensation dispute | The new event suffered a technical fault causing abnormal event points and badge counts, distorting rankings. The operator reset event data by 20:00 the same day, refunded live energy spent during the anomaly, set all items in the 'General' and 'Aoyagi Toya' exchange tabs to cost 1 badge each, and granted all players 1,000 free crystals and 20 large live-energy drinks as apology, with the event schedule postponed. In a TapTap community poll, 66% of participants rated the compensation as 'insufficient,' the main dispute being that the time cost of ranking players was not compensated. (Source: SMZDM and TapTap community reports)

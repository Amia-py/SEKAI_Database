【2024-06-12 Removal of certain songs following an incident involving a contributing creator】

Date: 2024-06-12
Event: Removal of certain songs following an incident involving a contributing creator
Subject: Japan

Citing the seriousness and social impact of an incident involving one of the project's song providers ('pusu'), the operation team removed the related songs from all services: deleting in-game song data and audio, unlisting the songs and MVs on the official channel, and taking down the 'SEKAI' versions from music platforms. Affected songs: 'Asagao no Chiru Koro ni' and 'Donna Ketsumatsu ga Onozomi dai?'.

(Source: official notice from the Traditional Chinese server operation team, June 2024)

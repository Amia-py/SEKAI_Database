【2020-09-05 Rehearsal Edit demo release】

Date: 2020-09-05
Event: Rehearsal Edit demo release
Subject: Japan


A pre-launch rhythm demo 'Rehearsal Edit' was released for a limited period (Sep 5-11), featuring three songs: 'Hibana -Reloaded-', 'Sweet Magic' and 'Tell Your World'. (Source: Sekaipedia public timeline)

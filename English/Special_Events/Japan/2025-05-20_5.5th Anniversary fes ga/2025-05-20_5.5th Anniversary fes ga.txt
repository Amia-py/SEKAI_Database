【2025-05-20 5.5th Anniversary fes gacha configuration error and compensation dispute】

Date: 2025-05-20
Event: 5.5th Anniversary fes gacha configuration error and compensation dispute
Subject: Japan

In the JP server's 5.5th Anniversary fes limited gacha, a configuration error prevented four past fes limited cards from appearing. Some players spent far beyond statistical probability without obtaining the desired card (the community nicknamed it the '0.007%' incident). After players inquired, the operator acknowledged the error but only distributed 3,000 crystals server-wide with no follow-up plan, prompting dissatisfaction. A producer's letter three days later focused on the circumstances and operational difficulties and stated that screening was too troublesome to handle, drawing further criticism, including from players on other servers.

(Source: community-compiled records, Moegirlpedia entry '0.007%=0%')

【2025-03-27 Mainland China server launch】

Date: 2025-03-27
Event: Mainland China server launch
Subject: Mainland_China


The Simplified Chinese version launched on Mar 27, 2025, about four years behind Japan; it started from the 3.5-anniversary build, skipping the first three years of events and gachas, with event stories viewable anytime and cards handled via official reruns. (Source: NGA community reports)

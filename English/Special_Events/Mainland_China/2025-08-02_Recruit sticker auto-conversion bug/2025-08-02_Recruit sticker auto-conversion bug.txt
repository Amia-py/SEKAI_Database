【2025-08-02 Recruit sticker auto-conversion bug】

Date: 2025-08-02
Event: Recruit sticker auto-conversion bug
Subject: Mainland_China


A serious bug auto-converted recruit stickers from gacha into wish shards. On Aug 2 officials announced affected stickers would be reissued and shards not reclaimed, with no extra compensation mentioned, drawing criticism. (Source: TapTap community reports)

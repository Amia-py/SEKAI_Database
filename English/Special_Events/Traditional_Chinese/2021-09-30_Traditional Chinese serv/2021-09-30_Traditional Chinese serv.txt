【2021-09-30 Traditional Chinese server launched in Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau by Ariel Network】

Date: 2021-09-30
Event: Traditional Chinese server launched in Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau by Ariel Network
Subject: Traditional_Chinese

The Traditional Chinese version for Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau launched on 2021-09-30, exactly one year after the JP server, already running game version 1.7. It is published by Ariel Network, serves only Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau in Traditional Chinese, and is officially referred to as the 'Asian Edition'. Pre-registrations exceeded 500,000.

【2024-06-12 Traditional Chinese server notice: removal of selected songs】

Date: 2024-06-12
Event: Traditional Chinese server notice: removal of selected songs
Subject: Traditional_Chinese

The Traditional Chinese operation team announced that, given the seriousness and social impact of an incident involving song provider 'pusu', the related songs would be removed: in-game data and audio deleted, official channel songs and MVs unlisted, and the 'SEKAI' versions taken down from music platforms. Affected songs: 'Asagao no Chiru Koro ni' and 'Donna Ketsumatsu ga Onozomi dai?'. The notice apologised to affected players.

(Source: official notice from the Traditional Chinese server operation team, June 2024)

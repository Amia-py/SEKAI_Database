【2021-09-30 Traditional Chinese server launch】

Date: 2021-09-30
Event: Traditional Chinese server launch
Subject: Traditional_Chinese


The Traditional Chinese version launched for Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau — exactly one year after the Japanese server. It was published/operated by Nuverse (ByteDance) under SEGA's regional licensing. (Source: SEGA press release, Sekaipedia/Fandom)

【2021-07-28 East-Asia publishing contract with Nuverse】

Date: 2021-07-28
Event: East-Asia publishing contract with Nuverse
Subject: SEGA


SEGA announced a publishing contract with Nuverse (ByteDance), entrusting operation of regions outside Japan in East Asia (Mainland China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau, Korea, SEA); the TW/KR/CN servers later launched under this structure. (Source: SEGA official press release)

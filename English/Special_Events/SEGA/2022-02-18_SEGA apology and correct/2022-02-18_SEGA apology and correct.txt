【2022-02-18 SEGA apology and correction over mini anime content】

Date: 2022-02-18
Event: SEGA apology and correction over mini anime content
Subject: SEGA

Following criticism of Petit SEKAI episode 6, SEGA issued a bilingual (Japanese/English) apology on its official account and withdrew the episode indefinitely; it was re-uploaded on 2022-03-15 after the relevant depictions were removed. This was the first case in which video content drew international criticism and a public apology from the publisher.

(Source: English Wikipedia, Controversy section)

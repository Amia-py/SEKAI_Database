## 数据来源

The new event suffered a technical fault causing abnormal event points and badge counts, distorting rankings. The operator reset event data by 20:00 the same day, refunded live energy spent during the anomaly, set all items in the 'General' and 'Aoyagi Toya' exchange tabs to cost 1 badge each, and granted all players 1,000 free crystals and 20 large live-energy drinks as apology, with the event schedule postponed. In a TapTap community poll, 66% of participants rated the compensation as 'insufficient,' the main dispute being that the time cost of ranking players was not compensated. (Source: SMZDM and TapTap community reports)
